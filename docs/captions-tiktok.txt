Free Mac app: a drum amp for your audio interface. Plug in, press the button, play through Motown, Nevermind, the gated 80s room. It tells you which way to turn the gain knob. Play any song on the Mac and it names the chords, the key and where the capo goes. Nothing recorded, nothing uploads. Get it: github.com/keithadler/ampmac
