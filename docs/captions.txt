Amp for Mac. Free, open source.
A drum amp for the input of the interface you already own: gate, punch, squash, tone, drive, room, thirty-nine sounds in twelve genres. It says which way to turn the gain knob.
The Ear (chordmap inside) names the chords, key and tempo of any song the Mac plays and where the capo goes. Nothing recorded, nothing leaves the Mac. Not a plugin; the delay is your interface's.
https://github.com/keithadler/ampmac
