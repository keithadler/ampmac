# Changelog

## 1.0.0, 2026-09-06

First release. A drum amp for the input of an audio interface: gate, attack and sustain shaper, compressor with parallel mix, three-band tone, drive, a room that can cut with the gate, and a limiter, with seventeen presets and your own saved beside them. A level guide says which way to turn the interface's gain knob and auto level trims the rest. The Ear listens to the Mac's own sound or an input, names the chords and the key, suggests capo positions, and keeps every listen. Command line for all of it, including offline rendering and chord reading of recordings.
